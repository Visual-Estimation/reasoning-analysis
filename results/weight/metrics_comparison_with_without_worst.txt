INTERPRETATION

KEY METRICS EXPLAINED:
- MAPE (Mean Absolute Percentage Error): Average % deviation from ground truth
- MAE (Mean Absolute Error): Average absolute deviation
- RMSE (Root Mean Square Error): Penalizes larger errors more heavily
- Median AE: Middle value of absolute errors (robust to outliers)
- Within ±X%: % of predictions within X% of ground truth
- Overestimate %: % of predictions that exceed ground truth
- Bias: Mean signed error (positive = consistent overestimation)
- Correlation: Strength of linear relationship between pred and truth
